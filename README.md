# Seven Seas — Board and Plunder Hostile Warships

Seven Seas 9minecraft Mod gives the ocean something to find. Sail far enough from land in vanilla Minecraft and the water becomes empty in a way no other biome is - hours of blue with nothing on the horizon. This puts five hand-built ships out there, abandoned and adrift, each one a proper multi-deck vessel rather than a decorative shell. They are meant to be boarded.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Aureljz**, **Zephyrusjz**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
